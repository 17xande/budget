AI access

An AI client — Claude Code, the Claude desktop app — can read the budget and categorise transactions through this server's MCP endpoint, acting as you: it can manage categories, rules, targets and account names. It cannot see or change members, passwords or tokens, import files, fetch from the bank, or delete transactions or categories. Every change it makes is listed under Activity, where a categorisation can be undone. Changing your password revokes all your tokens.
